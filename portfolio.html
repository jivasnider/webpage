Vehicle Aerodynamic Analysis

Aerodynamic analysis of an electric vehicle developed by our university's electric vehicle student community. The vehicle body was modelled in SolidWorks, followed by CFD simulations in ANSYS Fluent to investigate airflow velocity, pressure distribution, and aerodynamic performance. The analysis yielded a drag coefficient (Cd) of 0.428.
